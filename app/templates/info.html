About

What is Notella?

Notella is a student-focused study platform, combining note-taking with markdown toolbar, flashcard decks, and AI generated quizzes into a single workspace. Built to help students study easier.

How To

Using Notella

Notes
Creating a note Click the + button on the dashboard, enter a title, and you'll be taken to the note editor.
Markdown formatting Use the toolbar at the top of the editor or type Markdown directly. Supports headings, bold, italic, lists, code blocks, and tables.
Keyboard shortcuts Cmd+B bold, Cmd+I italic. Press Enter on a list item to continue the list, Tab to indent.
Tags Type a tag in the tags field and press Enter or comma to add it. Tags are shared across all users.
Visibility Notes are private by default. Toggle to Public to share them in the discovery page.
Saving Click Save or use the save button. The button turns red when there are unsaved changes.
Flashcards
Creating a deck Click + on the dashboard under the Flashcard Decks tab, enter a title, and add cards with a front and back.
Studying Click the play button on a deck to start a session. Flip cards and mark each one right or wrong to get a results summary.
AI Quiz
Generating a quiz Open a note and click Generate Quiz in the sidebar. The AI will read your note and generate multiple choice questions.
Quiz results The quizzes save your latest submitted results. However, they will be reset if you start a retake of the quiz.
Accuracy AI-generated questions may not always be accurate. Always verify answers with your course material.
Discover
Browsing Visit the Discover page to browse public notes and flashcard decks from other students. Filter by tag to narrow results.
Copying Click Copy on any public note or deck to add it to your own library. You can then edit it freely.
FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Is my data private?

Yes, your notes and flashcard decks are private by default. You can choose what to make public.

Can I copy someone else's notes?

Yes, you can copy and personalise any public notes and flashcard decks.

How does the AI quiz work?

The AI reads your note and automatically generates multiple choice questions. Results are not 100% accurate.

Are my quiz and flashcard session results saved?

The quizzes save your latest submitted results, however they will be reset if you start a retake of the quiz. The flashcard session results are not saved. They are just for self checking.

Can I delete my account?

Yes, go to your profile page and click Delete Account. This will permanently delete all your notes, decks, and quiz history.
